Our service

You may book a delivery via our website form, WhatsApp, or phone. To provide an accurate quote, we require the pickup location, drop-off location, and a description of the item(s) being delivered, including approximate size and weight.

Pricing & payment

Pricing for on-demand deliveries starts from KES 350 and varies based on pickup zone, drop-off zone, parcel size, and urgency. Business and recurring dispatch arrangements are priced individually and agreed in advance.

Payment is accepted via M-Pesa unless a corporate invoicing arrangement has been agreed in writing for repeat business clients. Payment is generally due before or at the point of pickup, unless otherwise agreed.
