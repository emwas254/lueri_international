Operating hours

Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM · Saturday, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM · Closed Sundays. Orders placed after 5:00 PM (or outside these hours) are queued for next-business-day dispatch.

FAQ

What does "International" in the name mean?

Lueri International is a Nairobi-born logistics and last-mile delivery company built with a global ambition. We provide reliable, professional, technology-driven delivery solutions that connect businesses with their customers efficiently and seamlessly. While our operations currently focus on Nairobi and its surrounding towns, our vision extends far beyond Kenya — we're building the systems, service standards, technology, and operational capacity required to scale into a trusted global logistics and delivery brand. Our immediate focus is to become a leading last-mile delivery partner in Nairobi, while laying the foundation for expansion across Kenya, Africa, and ultimately international markets. Our goal is simple: start local, build world-class, and go global.

What can and can't you deliver?

We carry parcels, documents, e-commerce orders, and personal items within Nairobi. We do not transport illegal goods, hazardous materials, cash, or items prohibited under Kenyan law. Fragile or high-value items should be declared at booking so we can confirm we can carry them safely.

How do I pay?

Most customers pay via M-Pesa after the quote is confirmed and before or at pickup. Repeat business clients can arrange invoicing — ask us when setting up a standing dispatch arrangement.

Is same-day delivery guaranteed?

No. We aim for same-day delivery on most Nairobi routes, but timeframes are estimates — not guarantees. Traffic, weather, and after-hours bookings can affect delivery time. We communicate delays as soon as we know about them.

What if my parcel is lost or damaged?

We take reasonable care of every item. Liability for loss or damage is limited to the lower of the declared value or KES 5,000 unless otherwise agreed in writing. See our Terms of Service for full details.

Do you offer business contracts?

Yes. If you dispatch daily or several times a week, we can set up a recurring pickup arrangement with agreed rates and a single point of contact. Apply for a business account and our team will confirm pickup details once we review your application.
