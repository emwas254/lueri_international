FAQ

How do I book a delivery?

Use the Book a Pickup form, or WhatsApp Lueri with your pickup location, drop-off location and parcel details.

How quickly can you deliver?

Same-day delivery is available for eligible Nairobi routes. Lueri confirms the route and pickup timing before dispatch.

How is pricing calculated?

Quotes depend on route, parcel details and urgency. Lueri confirms the price before pickup.

Do I need a Rewards account?

No. On-demand courier bookings do not require an account. Rewards members get additional benefits and points tracking.
