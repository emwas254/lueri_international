01

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Lueri International ("Lueri", "we", "us", "our"), a last-mile delivery and courier dispatch business operating in Nairobi, Kenya, collects, uses, and protects personal information belonging to customers, corporate clients, rewards members, and website visitors ("you").

This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service, which governs your use of our delivery services generally.

02

Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you interact with us:

WhenWhat we collect
Booking a deliveryName, phone number, pickup and drop-off addresses, item description
Joining Lueri RewardsFull name, phone number, and (optional) email address
Applying for a corporate accountCompany name, KRA PIN, business address, contact person's name, role, phone, and email, estimated delivery volume
Making a paymentPayment is processed by M-Pesa (and, where enabled, other licensed payment providers). We do not collect or store your M-Pesa PIN, card numbers, or bank account details ourselves.
Visiting our websiteA light/dark theme preference stored in your browser (see Section 9) — we do not run visitor tracking or advertising cookies

We collect this information directly from you, whether through our website forms, WhatsApp, or by phone.

03

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Confirm a quote, dispatch a rider, and complete your delivery
  • Contact you about the status of a booking, by phone or WhatsApp
  • Register and administer your Lueri Rewards membership, including calculating your points and tier
  • Review and set up corporate/business delivery accounts
  • Issue receipts and, where applicable, invoices
  • Improve our service and respond to complaints or claims

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

04

Our legal basis for processing

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, we rely on the following grounds to process your information:

  • Performance of a contract — to fulfil a delivery you've booked, or to administer your rewards or corporate account
  • Consent — where you've voluntarily provided information, such as an optional email address at Rewards sign-up
  • Legitimate business interest — for example, keeping records needed to handle a claim about a lost or damaged item
05

Lueri Rewards

Your phone number is your Rewards account identifier. When you or our staff look up your Rewards account, we return your name, tier, points balance, lifetime spend, and recent transaction history. We do not display your email address in these lookups.

To protect against abuse, lookups by phone number are rate-limited. Your tier is calculated from your total lifetime spend with us and does not reset over time.

Because a Rewards lookup only requires a phone number, please treat your registered phone number with the same care you would a password for that account — anyone who knows it can view your points and transaction history.
06

Who we share information with

We share information only where necessary to run our service:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — booking confirmations, dispatch updates, and corporate applications are sent as WhatsApp messages
  • M-Pesa / Safaricom and other licensed payment providers — to process payment for a delivery or Rewards transaction
  • Supabase, our database provider — stores Rewards and account data on our behalf, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Supabase does not use this data for its own purposes.
  • Our own riders and staff, to the extent needed to complete your delivery

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not sell it.

07

Storage & security

Your information is stored in a access-controlled database, protected by row-level security policies that restrict who and what can read or change it. Staff access to the Rewards console requires an individually approved account — there is no shared password.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, and we investigate promptly if we become aware of a potential issue.

08

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account or delivery history remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward to handle any claims, disputes, or legal obligations (such as tax record-keeping). If you ask us to delete your Rewards account, we will do so unless we are required to keep certain records by law.

09

Cookies & local storage

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only for:

  • Remembering your light/dark theme preference
  • Keeping staff logged in to the Rewards console between visits (an authentication session, not a copy of member data)
10

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to any legal obligation we have to retain it
  • Object to how we process your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

11

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging deliveries or business accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
